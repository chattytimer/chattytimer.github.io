Formula Reference

Quick reference for common scientific formulas

Speed = Distance / Time
v = d / t
Calculate velocity from distance and time
  • v = velocity (m/s)
  • d = distance (m)
  • t = time (s)
Acceleration
a = (v₂ - v₁) / t
Rate of change of velocity
  • a = acceleration (m/s²)
  • v₁ = initial velocity (m/s)
  • v₂ = final velocity (m/s)
Force
F = m × a
Newton's Second Law of Motion
  • F = force (N)
  • m = mass (kg)
  • a = acceleration (m/s²)
Work
W = F × d
Work done by a force
  • W = work (J)
  • F = force (N)
  • d = distance (m)
Kinetic Energy
KE = ½ × m × v²
Energy of motion
  • KE = kinetic energy (J)
  • m = mass (kg)
  • v = velocity (m/s)
Potential Energy
PE = m × g × h
Gravitational potential energy
  • PE = potential energy (J)
  • m = mass (kg)
  • g = 9.8 m/s²
  • h = height (m)
Density
ρ = m / V
Mass per unit volume
  • ρ = density (kg/m³)
  • m = mass (kg)
  • V = volume (m³)
Ohm's Law
V = I × R
Relationship between voltage, current, and resistance
  • V = voltage (V)
  • I = current (A)
  • R = resistance (Ω)
Molarity
M = n / V
Moles of solute per liter of solution
  • M = molarity (mol/L)
  • n = moles of solute
  • V = volume of solution (L)
Molar Mass
MM = m / n
Mass per mole of substance
  • MM = molar mass (g/mol)
  • m = mass (g)
  • n = moles
Percent Composition
% = (m_element / m_compound) × 100
Mass percentage of an element in a compound
pH
pH = -log[H⁺]
Measure of acidity or alkalinity
  • [H⁺] = hydrogen ion concentration (M)
  • pH < 7: acidic
  • pH = 7: neutral
  • pH > 7: basic
Enthalpy Change
ΔH = q / n
Heat change per mole of reactant
  • ΔH = enthalpy change (kJ/mol)
  • q = heat energy (kJ)
  • n = moles
Equilibrium Constant
K = [products] / [reactants]
Ratio of product to reactant concentrations at equilibrium
Half-Life
N = N₀ × (½)ⁿ
Radioactive decay calculation
  • N = remaining amount
  • N₀ = initial amount
  • n = number of half-lives
Photosynthesis
6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Process by which plants convert light into chemical energy
Cellular Respiration
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP
Process that releases energy from glucose
Hardy-Weinberg
p² + 2pq + q² = 1
Predicts allele frequencies in populations
  • p = frequency of dominant allele
  • q = frequency of recessive allele
Growth Rate
λ = Nt / N₀
Population growth rate per time period
  • λ = growth rate
  • Nt = population at time t
  • N₀ = initial population
Gene Frequency
Allele frequency = (2 × AA + Aa) / 2N
Calculate frequency of alleles in population
Metabolic Rate
MR = kcal / hr / kg
Energy expenditure per unit time and mass
